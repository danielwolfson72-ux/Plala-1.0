Everything between "a competitor moved" and "we reacted."

Tarifa is built for pricing and revenue teams who need clean data, not another dashboard to babysit.

Wide catalog coverage

Match your SKUs against competitor listings across marketplaces, D2C stores and comparison feeds, even when titles and variants don't line up.

Real-time change alerts

Get a Slack, email or webhook alert the moment a tracked price, stock status or promotion shifts — filtered to the changes that actually matter to you.

Repricing rules

Turn tracked data into action: set floor and ceiling rules and push updated prices straight to your PIM, ERP or storefront.

Historical trends

Every price is kept on record, so you can see how a category moved over a season and price ahead of it next time.

Clean, exportable data

Pull results through the API, sync to your warehouse, or export straight to a spreadsheet — no scraping cleanup required on your side.

Access controls

Bring your team in with roles for pricing, merchandising and finance, and single sign-on for larger organizations.
